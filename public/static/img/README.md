# Landing-page imagery — swap in your real photos here

The landing page loads photos **by role**. Replace these files with your own uploaded images
(same filename, `.jpg`) and the site updates automatically — no code changes needed.

| File            | Where it appears                          | Suggested subject                     | Aspect |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|--------|
| `hero.jpg`      | Hero, large right-hand photo              | A maker at work (e.g. home baker)     | 4:3 / 5:4 |
| `story-1.jpg`   | "Who Herizon is for" strip, card 1        | Home baking                           | 3:4 portrait |
| `story-2.jpg`   | Strip card 2 **and** hero inset thumbnail | Tailoring                             | 3:4 portrait |
| `story-3.jpg`   | Strip card 3                              | Handicrafts                           | 3:4 portrait |
| `story-4.jpg`   | Strip card 4                              | Food production                       | 3:4 portrait |
| `story-5.jpg`   | Strip card 5                              | Small local business / shop           | 3:4 portrait |
| `community.jpg` | "Community-focused" section               | Women working together                | 4:3 |

Tips: keep files under ~400 KB, at least 1200 px on the long edge; warm natural light photographs
read as most trustworthy. Captions/labels are in `public/static/app.js` → `views.landing()`.

**Current status:** placeholder files are in these slots pending the team's uploaded photos.
